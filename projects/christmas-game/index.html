Project 001 / In development

A family game, reimagined for three countries.

A Christmas tradition built around guessing prices from the Argos catalogue. Now an experiment in AI-assisted development and long-distance family connection.

Read the origin story ↗
Status
In development

Christmas 2026

The idea

A shared game screen shows a catalogue item, while family members in Seattle, Portugal, and the UK enter their price guesses from their phones.

What I'm exploring

Real-time synchronization, shared-screen interaction, mobile-first controllers, AI-assisted coding, and how to keep the whole experience simple enough for everyone to enjoy on Christmas Day.

The technology should serve the tradition—not replace what makes it special.

The playable game lives in a separate application and will be linked here when it is ready for visitors.
