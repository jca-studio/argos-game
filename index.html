About

A little about me.

I'm Jenna Clark. I've spent my career imagining better ways to do things and working with teams to turn those ideas into products, systems, and solutions that actually work.

AI is opening up new possibilities for building and experimenting more directly. But powerful tools are only part of the equation. Good ideas still need sound judgment, thoughtful design, and the discipline to make things work.

JCA Studio is where I explore those possibilities—sharing the projects, the process, what works, what doesn't, and what I learn along the way.
